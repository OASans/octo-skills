# Skill Guidance

1. **Include only what the agent needs to act.** Remove source history, provenance, and background that do not affect decisions or actions.
2. **Keep guidance simple and complete.** Use direct language, avoid repetition, and retain necessary conditions, exceptions, and examples.
3. **Add prohibitions only for concrete failure cases.** Keep deliberate safeguards that prevent known mistakes. Omit speculative warnings, obvious disclaimers, and language that mainly deflects responsibility.
